AgRoll: farm records in one place

A Python app that brings weather, crop records and staff payroll together for a farm.

When
Harvard CS50P, 2024
My role
Individual final project
Tools
Python, Dash, pytest

The idea

Farms track weather, yields, pests and workers in different places, often on paper. AgRoll puts them in one desktop app with three tabs: weather, fields and staff. In a full version, workers would clock in with biometrics and wages would be calculated automatically from hours worked.

What I built

Within the course scope, the app runs on simulated data: a hundred employees, crop yields and pest sightings over several months, plus live weather from an online API. It is written in object-oriented Python with a Dash interface, and tested with pytest.

The code is on GitHub, and the video demo walks through it.
